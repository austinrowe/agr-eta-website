About Eta Chapter

Who we are, who joins, who looks after the house, and who to ask.

Why we exist

Alpha Gamma Rho's purpose is to make better men, and through them a broader and better agriculture.

At Eta Chapter that means practice at leadership while you are a student at Iowa State and in the College of Agriculture and Life Sciences, with a steady push on grades and on how you carry yourself. Our alumni are innovators, leaders and educators in agriculture.

We are a social fraternity and a professional one. Members in the same career field form study groups, and officer roles give you real leadership practice before you graduate.

Questions people ask

Does it cost to join?
Yes. Members pay rent to live in the house and chapter dues while they live there. The money runs the house and funds chapter activities. Current rent and dues: [add amounts]. Scholarships, loans and tutoring help are on the Join page.
When can I join?
Recruitment starts when you are a junior or senior in high school. The interest form is open any time.
Do I have to be an ag major?
No. We take men going into agriculture, food or fiber, including food science, biotechnology, agri-marketing and environmental science.
Do I need a 4-H or FFA background?
No. We have strong ties to both, but you don't need to have been in either.
Is there pledging?
No. Everyone who joins is a member from the start. The chapter reviews potential members in advance.
Where is the house?
201 Gray Avenue, about a ten-minute walk from most classrooms.

Our house mom

Diane Pinneke grew up on a farm and spent her career as a child, adolescent and family therapist. When she retired she chose to be a house mother at an Iowa State fraternity, and she chose AGR after seeing what it did for her nephew.

She teaches etiquette for social and professional settings, proofreads résumés and applications, writes newsletters to parents, and hosts Founder's Day, Homecoming and Parent Weekend.
