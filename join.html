Who joins

Men headed into agriculture, food or fiber. That includes food science, biotechnology, agri-marketing and environmental science. You don't need a farm background, and you don't need 4-H or FFA.

There is no pledging. Everyone who joins is a member from the start.

Scholarships

Nearly $100,000 a year, available only to Eta members. Alumni gifts and endowments fund them through the ETA Educational Foundation, the Iowa State University Foundation and the AGR National Educational Foundation.

Ask our VNR of Scholarship, Logan Brand, for the details.

Student loans

Up to $5,000 per member from the ETA Educational Foundation. No interest while you are enrolled at Iowa State, and 1% for the first two years after, which is when the loan is meant to be repaid. After two years the rate is 12%.

Applications go to the Foundation treasurer, Mike Nissly, and a student loan committee reviews them.

Tutoring

The Foundation pays back half the cost of tutoring. Send your receipts to the Foundation treasurer, Mike Nissly.

Achievement grants

Twice a year, at Homecoming and Founder's Day, undergraduates get grants for leadership, growth, grades and what they give back to AGR and the college. Over $557,000 has gone to 239 members since 2014.

For parents

The house is about a ten-minute walk from most classrooms. Our house mother, Diane Pinneke, writes newsletters to parents and hosts Parent Weekend. About 1,500 alumni are still living, many of them in agriculture, and some of them take on a younger member through the Alumni Dad Program.
