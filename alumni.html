Alumni Dad Program

We pair two or three new initiates with an alumnus who shares their major or career interests. It started in fall 2022 with 15 alumni, each paired with two new initiates.

Alumni help with résumés, elevator pitches and mock interviews before the Iowa State career fair, and some host dinners at 201 Gray. Near the end of spring semester we also look for company reps and farm owners around Ames and Des Moines to host an industry tour.

The Legacy Fund

Started in 2014 with the Iowa State Foundation and the College of Agriculture and Life Sciences. It has paid out over $557,000 in grants to 239 members, awarded twice a year to undergraduates for leadership and academic achievement.

Give once or monthly through Iowa State Foundation, to either the expendable AGR Achievement Award or the endowed AGR Class Gift Fund.

isu.foundation/agr

Name an award

A gift or five-year pledge of $50,000 or more, from a person or a group such as a new initiate class, creates a named award, such as the "John Smith Alpha Gamma Rho Achievement Award." It can be endowed or expendable.

A planned estate gift of $50,000 or more, with $1,000 or more given each year, also qualifies.
